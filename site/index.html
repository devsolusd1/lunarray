NFO how it works

    

      

        
market. the engine is live from block one: while the token sits on the pons curve it reads the curve's reserves and buys back through the curve. at graduation it switches to the uniswap v4 pool by itself. nothing is redeployed.

        
oracle. one price sample per epoch. target = mean of the last samples. nothing off-chain, nothing signed.

        
flywheel. 5% creator tax → 3% straight to the treasury, 2% to the engine → half to stakers as ETH, half to buybacks → tokens into the crypt → bonds paid. every step is a public function.

      

      

        
safety. immutable addresses, curve and pool. the guardian can only pause new entries for up to 7 days, propose parameters that execute 48h later, and sweep idle protocol funds after 30 days without bonds. stakes and rewards are never touchable.

        
keepers. poke, harvest, settle and start are permissionless. the poke caller is tipped from the reserve.
